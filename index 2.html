Experimental principles

No scales, chords, melody goals, or beauty rewards. Organisms perceive frequencies, choose what to attend to, learn short sequences, imitate or ignore others, and sometimes reproduce. Groups are detected from proximity and shared behaviour; they are not preassigned.

This is a computational artificial-life experiment, not evidence of consciousness or philosophical free will. Early versions use simplified physics and learning.
